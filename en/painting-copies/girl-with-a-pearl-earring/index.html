Photographic documentation

The gallery presents photographs documenting the creation of a painted copy of Johannes Vermeer’s “Girl with a Pearl Earring”.

The photographs show broad areas of colour, a reproduction of the original used as a reference, close-ups of the face and clothing, and the completed composition.

Each photograph can be enlarged and viewed in the full-screen gallery.

About the original

“Girl with a Pearl Earring” by Johannes Vermeer

Johannes Vermeer’s original was painted around 1665 and is often called the “Mona Lisa of the North”. The painting is held at the Mauritshuis museum in The Hague.
